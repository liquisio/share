A lower-cost way to run subscription sites on Wix.

I’m looking for a handful of agency partners to try an early build. Honest feedback over polished launch — happy to talk through fit before you commit.

The idea

Wix Pricing Plans is great, but it carries Wix’s transaction fees and locks subscription logic into Wix’s billing. This app keeps the editor experience you already know, and moves the money to Stripe.

Stripe handles payments and subscriptions. The app syncs subscription status back into Wix. Member roles are assigned automatically — so any role-based logic your sites already use keeps working untouched.
